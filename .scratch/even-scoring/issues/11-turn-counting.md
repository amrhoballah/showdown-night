# Which games count turns, and what triggers the uneven-turns warning?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Hussein Rzikana
Blocked by:

## Question

The host is warned, but allowed to proceed, when ending a game with uneven turns ("What happens when turns are uneven at game end?"). Which games have turns at all? Act It Out and Wavelength rotate teams; Outburst picks a team per round from a dropdown; Emoji and Jeopardy are open buzz-in. For each game with turns, what counts as one turn and what exact condition triggers the warning? How does this work in free-for-all?

## Answer

**Three games have turns, a turn is a confirmed round, and any imbalance warns.** Resolved by grilling, 2026-09-28.

- **Games with turns:** Wavelength, Act It Out, Outburst. Emoji and Jeopardy are open buzz-in and have none; a Daily Double is not a turn.
- **Outburst is one team per round:** the team whose turn it is shouts; the credited team is that team. The narration changes from "everyone else calls out" to "your team calls out".
- **What counts as a turn:** a round whose result the host confirms, including a 0-point result. Act It Out's "Another turn" (discard) is a do-over, not a turn.
- **Warning trigger:** ending a turn-based game when not every entity has the same number of turns (most − fewest ≥ 1). The warning names who is short, e.g. "El Captains: 2 turns, the others: 3. End anyway?"
- **Preselecting the next turn:** all three games preselect the entity with the fewest turns (ties → next in order); the host can override.
- **Free-for-all:** same rule per player.
- **Score edits** never change turn counts.
- **Outburst's natural finish** is the last complete lap: 8 rounds for 2 or 4 teams, 9 for 3.
- **Outburst is not a free-for-all game:** one person shouting alone isn't Outburst, and 9 categories can't give 12 players a turn.
