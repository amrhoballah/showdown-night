# What ends a game?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Nothing in the app ends a game today: Act It Out and Wavelength loop turn after turn, Emoji offers "Reshuffle and go again", Outburst stops after its last category, a Jeopardy board keeps its progress when you leave, and Home ends nothing. Placement points are only awarded when a game ends, so what ends one?

## Answer

Both. A host-pressed **End game** action is always available and ends the game on the spot. Games with a natural finish (Emoji deck used up, last Outburst category, Final Jeopardy settled) also *prompt* the host to end the game there. Ending shows the final game scores and awards placement points.
