# What does the sticky scoreboard show during a game?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

During a game, does the sticky scoreboard show the night standings, the current game scores, or both?

## Answer

**Both**: the current game scores and the night standings together. How that lays out on a TV is open: see "How does the scoreboard show game scores and night standings together on a TV?".
