SHOWDOWN NIGHT

Game night · 12 players

Pick sides, then pick a game.

Set up how your crew is split, then launch either board from here. Koshary-break optional. Scores carry across both games, so the trivia round and the shout-it-out round feed one final leaderboard.

How are you splitting up?

Add each player. Tap their name any time during the games to give a point.

Game 1 · 60 questions · EN + عربي

Jeopardy

Two full boards — one English, one Arabic with its own native categories (أكمل المثل, طرب, سينما). Six categories × five values each, a hidden Daily Double with real wagering, and a Final Jeopardy where every team bets part of its score.

Game 2 · 9 rounds · ~30 min

Outburst Rounds

A mystery category appears — from ice cream flavors to Cairo Uber drivers — the clock starts, and everyone shouts out as many matches as they can before time's up. The hidden list reveals after — tally what you actually said.

Game 3 · 12 players · ~40 min

Mafia

The screen plays narrator. Pass the laptop once for secret roles, then it runs every night phase, the day timer, the vote, and the reveal — and tells you when one side has won.

Game 4 · ~15 min

Act It Out

One guesser sits with their back to the TV. Everyone else sees the word and has 60 seconds to act, describe, or mime their way through as many as they can.

Game 5 · ~15 min

Emoji Riddles

Films, songs, places, and dishes spelled out entirely in emoji. Fast, loud, and readable from the far end of the room — good for catching your breath between the heavy games.

Game 6 · ~20 min

Wavelength

A spectrum runs 1 to 10 across the screen — overrated to underrated, waste of money to worth every pound. One player secretly sees the number and gives a single clue; their team argues, then taps a number.
